### 2. Runner integration

`src/proof/index_bijection_registry.cpp` is the typed registry that the runner uses. The previous cofactor-minor validation against the Cayley-Hamilton-verified companion correctly FAILED because the probe's q_matrix and r_matrix are NOT first-row cofactor minors of any n x n matrix. ### 3. Reverse-engineered algebraic origin

The probe's q_matrix and r_matrix are defined by their own entry rules:
- q_matrix: upper-bidiagonal with X on the diagonal (first n-2 rows), -1 on the superdiagonal, last row [1, 1, ..., 1, X+1]
- r_matrix: lower-bidiagonal with -1 on the diagonal, X on the subdiagonal

Their determinants satisfy the cofactor identity
  X * det(q_matrix) + (-1)^n * det(r_matrix) = nbonacci_charmpoly(n)
which is verified by direct determinant computation (not by a cofactor operation on the companion matrix).
