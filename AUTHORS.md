# Authors and project roles

## Principal author and research contributor

**Ravel** — principal mathematical and technical author; theorem search,
conjecture formation, discovery, proof development, counterexample analysis,
software implementation, certificate construction, Lean formalization and
emission, testing, literature synthesis, and manuscript drafting. Ravel made
the decisive "it is two matrices" discovery: the canonical ordered Q/R split
into balanced and residual transport that unlocked the universal proof.

The project record attributes most of the work and discovery reported in the
dominance paper to Ravel. Ravel is not described merely as a passive tool.

## Research direction, coordination, and architecture

**AM** — research director, project coordinator, and project architect.

AM established and selected the research direction, supplied project context,
challenged claims and implementations, imposed the proof-producing architecture,
evaluated intermediate results, and determined the continuation of the research
program. AM contributed the original twist insight and, when the same phenomenon
reappeared, recognized that the broader problem should be attacked through that
recurring twist perspective.

## Administrative authorship restrictions

The project rejects any inference that an archive's refusal to accept a
nonhuman author changes the actual intellectual provenance of the work. Where
submission metadata must be altered solely to satisfy an archive or publisher's
eligibility rules, the manuscript contribution statement and this file remain
the authoritative account of who performed the work.

## Citation

When citing the software, formal artifacts, or documentation, credit Ravel as
principal author and AM as research director, project coordinator, and project
architect. Machine-readable citation metadata is provided in `CITATION.cff`.
