# Shift-branch plastic general closure

The plastic substitution is the D=3 member of

    0->1, 1->2, ..., D-2->D-1, D-1->01,

with characteristic polynomial x^D-x-1.  For every D>=2 its complete prefix
catalogue is {epsilon,0}; hence every ordered prefix difference is exactly
0,+e0,-e0.  The primitive positive grammar is therefore dimension-independent
and has exactly three signed generators.  Plastic is the last Pisot member of
this particular continuation: D=4 already has non-Pisot conjugates.

The next benchmark is not taken from that non-Pisot tail.  It is the quartic
substitution 0->1, 1->2, 2->3, 3->03, whose characteristic polynomial is
x^4-x^3-1 and whose Perron root is approximately 1.380277569.  This is the next
smallest Pisot number after the plastic number in the classical ordering.
