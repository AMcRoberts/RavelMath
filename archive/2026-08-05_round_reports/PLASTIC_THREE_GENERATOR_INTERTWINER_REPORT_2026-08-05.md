# Plastic three-generator intertwiner

For the plastic substitution

\[
a\mapsto b,\qquad b\mapsto c,\qquad c\mapsto ab,
\]

the exact contact-boundary graph has the three primitive prefix-defect
classes \(G_0,G_+,G_-\).  The comparison object is the universal ordered
parent-role graph on the nine states \((i,j)\in\{a,b,c\}^2\).

Every ordered pair of parent decompositions contributes one universal edge,
labelled by the exact prefix-abelianization difference

\[
0,\quad +a,\quad -a.
\]

The concrete boundary graph is a deletion-only subgrammar.  The comparison
map forgets displacement:

\[
[i,x,j]\longmapsto(i,j).
\]

With its 0/1 incidence matrix \(P\), exact entrywise multiplication proves

\[
G_0^{B}P\leq PG_0^{U},\qquad
G_+^{B}P\leq PG_+^{U},\qquad
G_-^{B}P\leq PG_-^{U}.
\]

The concrete graph has 101 states and 125 edges.  The universal parent-pair
catalogue has nine states and sixteen edges.  Every concrete edge has a
universal witness of the same ordered source role, target role, and generator
class.

This is the first completed comparison in the project whose primitive positive
grammar has three generators rather than a Q/R specialization.  The generic
finite-positive-grammar theorem therefore propagates the three simultaneous
inequalities through every positive noncommutative word polynomial and every
norm-weighted bounded operator decoration.
