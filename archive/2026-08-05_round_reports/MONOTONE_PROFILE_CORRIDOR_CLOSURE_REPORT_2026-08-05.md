# Monotone-profile corridor closure

For every `D >= 2` and `0 <= k <= D-1`, define `a(D,k)=(2^k,1^{D-k})`. The implementation derives the complete parent catalogue directly from `i -> 0^{a_i}(i+1)`, proves that every paired prefix channel is `Q`, `R`, or `R^2`, and records that exact target solving and contact-boundary membership only delete catalogue channels. Subdivision of `R^2` gives the same primitive `Q/R` parent-role renewal suspension and comparison map. Thus the entire corridor from n-bonacci (`k=0`) to uniform `m=2` generalized multinacci (`k=D-1`) is symbolically closed for all `(D,k)`.

Exact identities: zero-parent occurrences `D+k`; total parent occurrences `2D+k-1`; maximum roof `1` at `k=0`, otherwise `2`; primitive positive alphabet exactly `{Q,R}`.
